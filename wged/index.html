02 / About

One engineer, a broad network, and a direct approach.

Wim Geerits · Founder

Hello, I'm Wim Geerits — founder of WGED, based in Peer, Belgium. I graduated in 2006 as an automotive engineer specialising in product design.

My journey began in the RV industry, followed by 17 years as a design engineer at Hegge ID (2007–2024), working across machine construction, agriculture, transport, festival equipment, healthcare, interior design, and wood & gas heating systems.

At WGED I combine that broad industry experience with deep technical know-how to offer customised engineering. I work with a wide network of trusted suppliers — from prototyping partners to full-scale production — so I can guide projects efficiently from concept to manufacturing.

The goal is always the same: smart, practical, visually refined solutions. I'm always open to new collaborations — let's connect.
